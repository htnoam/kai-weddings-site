Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

KAI Events ("KAI", "we", "us") plans destination weddings in Cyprus, Israel, Greece, Italy, France and worldwide. This policy explains what personal details we collect through this website, why we collect them, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you fill in a contact form on this website, we collect the details you choose to share with us:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • Anything you tell us about your wedding plans

We also collect basic, anonymous usage information (such as pages visited and device type) through analytics tools, to understand how the website is used and improve it.

How we use your details

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your wedding
  • To send you a proposal or information you asked for
  • To keep in touch with relevant updates, if you agreed to this when submitting the form

We do not sell your details, and we never share them with third parties for their own marketing.

Who processes your details

We use trusted service providers to run this website and receive your enquiries, including our website host, our form-processing service, and Google analytics and tag management tools. They process your details only on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service.

If you go on to plan a wedding with us, we may share necessary details (such as names and dates) with the venues and suppliers involved in your event.

Cookies

This website uses cookies and similar technologies for analytics and to measure the performance of our advertising. You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings.

How long we keep your details

We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to respond to you and handle any resulting planning work, and for any period required by law. After that, we delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • See the personal details we hold about you
  • Correct or update them
  • Delete them
  • Stop sending you updates

Just email us and we'll take care of it. If you are in the EU, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Contact us

For any question about this policy or your personal details, email htnoam@gmail.com.
